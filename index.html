ABOUT CASEFILE

The pool changes.
The history remains.

Advance the case one step at a time, or press Play. Select a source, lead, or decision to inspect its recorded relationships. Return to an earlier step to see the information available in this replay at that point.

Watch for the moment three reports turn out to share an origin. Later, an investigator reassesses their identification, while a separate lead remains available.

Every person, organization, place, report, and outcome in the case is fictional. This is a scripted companion demonstration, with no connection to an operational case or police system.

Assessments represent recorded human judgments in the scenario. Source links alone establish neither truth nor independence. The downloadable JSON-LD uses W3C PROV terms alongside example-specific assessment terms. It is not a certified CASE/UCO implementation or a tamper-proof ledger.

This standalone demo does not change the existing research paper, application, or lecture files. See the repository for the presenter walkthrough and model limits.
